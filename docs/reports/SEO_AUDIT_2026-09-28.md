## A. Executive summary

**Overall SEO health: 59/100.** This is the auditor's weighted score, using the claude-seo weights:

| Area | Score |
|---|---|
| Technical | 55 |
| Content | 50 |
| On-page | 60 |
| Schema | 55 |
| Performance (lab) | 75 |
| AI readiness | 65 |
| Images | 80 |

Security issues were found and are reported separately, because they outrank SEO.

**The site's current position.**
- The B2B/MICE long tail works. Observed Google positions (gl=ma):

| Query | Position |
|---|---|
| professional congress organizer morocco | #1 |
| sustainable events morocco | #3 |
| events production morocco | #5 |
| incentive travel morocco (through a blog post) | #9 |

- Morocco Quest was **not seen in the top ~20 for any B2C commercial query**, and not seen for "morocco dmc", "dmc maroc" or "destination management company morocco".
- "dmc marrakech" sits around position 35, from repo scrape data dated 2026-08-26.
- The main limits are product/intent fit (no Sahara 2–3-day product, no prices), trust signals, taxonomy bloat, and several live technical defects. Keyword choice is not the main limit.

**Critical, confirmed on production:**
1. **The whole `.git` directory is publicly downloadable.** `/.git/HEAD`, `/.git/config`, `/.git/index` (119 KB) and `/.git/logs/HEAD` all return 200, so anyone can reconstruct the source code and its history.
   - Also public: `LEADS_AUDIT_REPORT.md` (business lead data), `docs/reports/*.md`, `composer.json`, `problemes.md`, `seo_audit.py`, `clear_opcache.php` (executable), `vendor/composer/installed.json`, `urllist.txt` and `readme.html`.
2. **Reflected XSS on `/search`.** `?query=` is echoed unescaped into `<meta name="keywords">`. A harmless `<b>` marker came back as live markup (§D-1).
3. **Spam comments are published instantly and are indexable.** About 52 of the 60 blog comments are spam. They include violent extremist threats, escort ads and casino ads. The comment endpoints have no moderation.
4. **GA4 records no page views.** The GTM container `GTM-WVCGDJ98` publishes zero tags, and the direct `gtag('config','G-QXBGN7DKMB',{send_page_view:false})` suppresses page views. Selenium saw **no `/g/collect` hit** in 10 seconds on `/` and `/tours`.
5. **Two HTTP 500s:** `/blog/search?query=…` and `/tours?page=N` for any N ≥ 2.
6. **The "31 schema errors" are reproduced exactly:**
   - 23 activities use `offers` on `TouristAttraction`.
   - 8 tours use `duration` on `TouristTrip`.
   - All 31 also publish a price in structured data while the page shows "Price On Request".
7. **Page-cache privacy bug (code-confirmed).** After a failed form, the next cached render can contain one visitor's `old()` name, email and phone, and serve it to everyone for 6 hours.

**Biggest opportunities:**
- A Sahara hub plus a 2–3-day Merzouga product with visible prices.
- An Aït Benhaddou day trip and an Agafay dinner.
- Clear up the ~100 thin tag and category pages.
- Fix the activity, destination and blog internal linking.
- Add verifiable B2B trust signals: licence number, named clients, memberships, case studies.
- Merge the TripAdvisor duplicates and optimise the Google Business Profile.
- French versions of the DMC pages (evidence-backed).

**Major limitations:**
- No Search Console data: the service account can only see local-morocco-tours.com.
- No Semrush, Ahrefs or Moz data.
- No CrUX field data, because traffic is too low.
- Common Crawl lookup failed.
- TripAdvisor blocks bots.

## H. Content audit

**Blog**
- **Spam (P0).** About 52 of the 60 comments are spam. The eclipse post renders 12,311 words, of which about 505 are the article. Comments publish instantly through `CommentController@store` and `BlogController@replyToComment`, which have no moderation, captcha or rate limit.
