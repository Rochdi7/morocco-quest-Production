## 1. Executive summary

The pricing is **inverted compared with the market**:

- **The activities and day trips are mostly priced high.** 15 of the 23 are 25% or more above what competitors charge for a comparable private experience. Several cost 2–3 times as much (Agadir, Casablanca, Volubilis & Meknes, Rabat food tour, hammam).
- **Four multi-day tours are priced low.** *Royal Cities 6-Day* ($900) and *5-Day Marrakech & Sahara* ($786) are **27–29% below** comparable private tours on TourRadar. *9-Day Rail* and *8-Day Cultural* are also below the market.

| Position vs. market | Activities (23) | Multi-day tours (8) |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 Well above (≥25% over the typical price) | **15** | 0 |
| 🟠 Upper end of the range (still credible) | 4 | 0 |
| 🟢 In line with the market | 4 | 4 |
| 🔵 Below the market (room to raise) | 0 | **4** |

**What this means:**

1. Travellers comparing a $119 Agadir half-day with $29–$70 alternatives mostly won't book. The overpriced day activities are probably costing conversions.
2. The multi-day tours bring the most revenue per booking, and four of them sell below the market. Raising *Royal Cities* and *Sahara 5-Day* just to the market floor would add about **$200 per person** on each.
3. **Suggested strategy:** bring overpriced activities down to the upper-middle of the market, and raise underpriced tours to at least the bottom of the market. This rebalances revenue rather than cutting it.

> All suggested prices are based on the market only. Check them against your own costs and margins before you change anything in the admin.

## 2. Method

1. **Your prices** were read from each live product page on 24 Nov 2026 (the admin `price_adult`, USD, per adult). Inclusions (hotels, meals, group size) for the multi-day tours come from the same pages.
2. **Competitor prices** are for the **same route or experience in the private format**, from online travel agencies and marketplaces, local operators, and premium specialists. Each one is linked in section 4.
3. **The "typical" price** is the middle of the credible private listings. These were excluded from the typical price:
   - shared/group tours
   - transport-only transfers
   - obvious mislistings
4. **Position bands:**
   - **Well above:** ≥25% over the typical price.
   - **Upper end:** above the typical price but inside the observed range.
   - **In line:** within about ±15% of the typical price.
   - **Below:** under the typical price by more than 15%.

**Limits of this data:**
- These are "from" prices. Final prices depend on date, season and group size.
- Online travel agency prices include a 20–30% commission, so a competitor's direct price is often **lower** than listed. The gap on overpriced activities is probably larger than shown.
- Conversions are approximate: €1 ≈ $1.08, £1 ≈ $1.27, NZ$1 ≈ $0.58.

2. **Say what is included next to the price:** mosque, Volubilis and Majorelle tickets, lunch, licensed guide, first-class train. Including these justifies a price above the market.
