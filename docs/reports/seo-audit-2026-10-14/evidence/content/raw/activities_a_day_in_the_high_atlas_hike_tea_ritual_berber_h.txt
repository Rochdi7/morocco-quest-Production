Overview
Escape the bustle of Marrakech on this
Atlas Mountains day trip
and journey into the heart of the High Atlas for an unforgettable day of cultural immersion and natural beauty. After a scenic drive to the peaceful village of Imlil, you'll be welcomed by Lahcen, a local mountain guide whose deep knowledge and storytelling bring the Berber way of life to light. Enjoy a gentle one-hour hike through terraced valleys, traditional hamlets, and mule trails, with meaningful encounters and insights along the way. The walk ends at a traditional Berber home, where you'll experience a ceremonial mint and herbal tea followed by a delicious, home-cooked, three-course lunch made from seasonal mountain ingredients. In the late afternoon, return to Marrakech with a heart full of connection and discovery.

Hour 01:
                                                                DETAILED PROGRAM
Today, we trade the buzz of Marrakech for the cool, invigorating air of the High Atlas Mountains.
After breakfast, we’ll journey south on a scenic 1.5-hour drive to Imlil, a peaceful village cradled by dramatic peaks and terraced valleys. Here, you'll be welcomed by Lahcen, our passionate and knowledgeable local mountain guide whose roots run deep in these mountains. More than a guide, Lahcen is a storyteller—his insight will open a window into the rich traditions and way of life of the local Berber communities.
Your gentle 1-hour hike is more than a walk—it’s a cultural immersion. You’ll pass through charming Berber hamlets, catch glimpses of rural life, and walk past stone-built houses, almond groves, and narrow mule trails etched into the hillsides. Along the way, Lahcen will introduce you to villagers, point out native herbs, and share stories that bring the landscape to life.
The hike culminates at a traditional Berber home, where you’ll be welcomed with a freshly prepared mint and herbal tea, served in a warm, ceremonial style. More than just a beverage, this tea is a cultural symbol—Lahcen will explain its deep roots in Moroccan hospitality and the use of local herbs not only for flavor, but for healing and community bonding. This moment is intimate, meaningful, and utterly Moroccan.
After this enriching pause, you'll be served a home-cooked, 3-course lunch featuring seasonal, mountain-grown ingredients. A highlight is the lovingly prepared vegetarian couscous, rich in texture and flavor—a true taste of Atlas mountain cuisine.
In the late afternoon, we make our way back to Marrakech. Guests will be dropped off at their hotel or riad, carrying with them a deeper understanding of the mountains, the people, and the timeless rhythms of Berber life.
