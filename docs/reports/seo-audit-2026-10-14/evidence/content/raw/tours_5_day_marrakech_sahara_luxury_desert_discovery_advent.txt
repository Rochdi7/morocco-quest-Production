Day 01 :
                                                                Arrival in Marrakech – City Tour & Welcome Dinner
Upon arrival at Marrakech Menara Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by our local representative and transferred to your hotel or riad. In the afternoon, embark on an insightful guided tour of Marrakech that begins with a visit to Dar El Bacha, a stunning palace-turned-museum showcasing exquisite Moroccan craftsmanship and decorative arts. From there, dive into the medina's hidden corners with a curated Hidden Gems Tour: visit a Fondouks (traditional inn), a public bakery still in use by local families, and an age-old furnace that powers hammams and neighborhood ovens. Continue into the vibrant souks, where artisans weave, carve, and dye using age-old techniques passed down through generations. As evening falls, enjoy a refined Moroccan dining experience at Dar Zellij, one of the city's most atmospheric restaurants housed in a beautifully restored riad. The setting, with its candlelit ambiance and intricate Zellij tilework, is the perfect welcome to Morocco.
Day 02 :
                                                                Marrakech – Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou – Dades Valley
After breakfast, depart Marrakech and travel across the majestic High Atlas Mountains via the scenic Tizi n’Tichka Pass (2,260 meters), offering breathtaking views of Berber villages and rugged peaks. Along the way, stop to explore the legendary Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and an extraordinary example of southern Moroccan architecture that has served as the backdrop for numerous films and series. Continue your journey through Ouarzazate, often called the "Gateway to the Sahara," and the fragrant Valley of Roses, before reaching the dramatic Dades Valley, carved by centuries of wind and water erosion into a spectacular canyon landscape. Enjoy the evening in a charming kasbah or guesthouse nestled in this awe-inspiring setting.
Day 03 :
                                                                Dades – Todgha Gorges – Erfoud – Merzouga Desert Camp
Following breakfast, head towards the desert, passing through the striking Todgha Gorges—massive limestone cliffs towering up to 300 meters high—perfect for a short walk and photo stop. Journey on through Erfoud, a town known for its fascinating fossil workshops and Saharan culture. In Erfoud, trade your minibus for 4x4 Jeeps for an exhilarating off-road adventure through the dunes. At the foot of the vast Erg Chebbi dunes, mount your camel for a sunset trek across the golden sand—an unforgettable Sahara moment. Upon arrival at your luxury desert camp, enjoy a traditional dinner beneath the stars, accompanied by local music and storytelling around the campfire.
Day 04 :
                                                                Merzouga – Return to Marrakech
Wake up early to experience the serenity of a Sahara sunrise as it paints the dunes in hues of gold and rose. After breakfast, begin your journey to Marrakech, passing again through Erfoud, Ouarzazate, and the scenic Atlas Mountains. The day is long but filled with diverse landscapes—from stony desert plains to snow-capped peaks. Along the way, enjoy rest stops for lunch, refreshments, and photography.
Day 05 :
                                                                Marrakech – Souks Tour & Departure
Enjoy a final morning in Marrakech with a guided walk through the medina’s vibrant souks, where stalls overflow with spices, lanterns, leather goods, and textiles. This is a wonderful opportunity to soak in the atmosphere and pick up last-minute treasures. Depending on your flight time, you will be transferred to Marrakech Menara Airport for your departure, carrying unforgettable memories of Morocco’s soul-stirring landscapes and cultural richness.

Marrakech : Grand Plaza Marrakech
Grand Plaza Marrakech is a modern 4-star hotel ideally located in Hivernage, opposite the train station and near key attractions like Jemaa El Fna. It features stylish rooms, a large pool, spa, gym, and several dining options—perfect for both business and leisure travelers exploring Marrakech.
Dades: Xaluca Hotel
Hotel Xaluca Dades is a luxurious 4-star retreat nestled at 1,612 meters in the High Atlas Mountains, offering panoramic views of the Dades Valley. This hotel features 106 rooms and suites adorned with African-inspired decor, each equipped with modern amenities such as flat-screen satellite TVs, Wi-Fi, minibars, air conditioning, and heating. Guests can unwind in the outdoor pool with a Jacuzzi, indulge in the spa and hammam, or stay active with facilities like a fitness center, tennis court, and petanque area. The on-site restaurant serves a variety of Moroccan and international dishes in an elegant dining room with panoramic views. Located just 20 km from the Dades Gorges, Hotel Xaluca Dades is an ideal base for exploring the region's natural beauty and Berber culture.
Dades: Madu Luxury Camp
Madu Luxury Desert Camp, nestled in the golden dunes of Erg Chebbi near Merzouga, offers an exceptional glamping experience in the heart of the Sahara. Each of the 15 spacious tents features traditional Saharan décor, private bathrooms with hot showers, air conditioning, and heating, ensuring comfort in every season. Guests can savor Moroccan cuisine under the stars, enjoy live Berber music by the campfire, and partake in activities like camel treks, sandboarding, and 4x4 desert excursions. With its blend of authentic charm and modern amenities, Madu Luxury Desert Camp provides an unforgettable desert retreat.
