Overview
Soar above the breathtaking landscapes of Marrakech on a magical hot air balloon ride at sunrise. Drift peacefully over palm groves, Berber villages, and the Atlas Mountains as the morning light paints the sky. After landing, enjoy a traditional Berber breakfast in a serene countryside setting before receiving your flight certificate and returning to your hotel. A once-in-a-lifetime experience combining adventure, culture, and unforgettable views.

Hour 01:
                                                                DETAILED PROGRAM
4:30–5:30 AM – Hotel Pickup
Your adventure begins with an early morning pickup from your accommodation in Marrakech. You’ll be transported in a comfortable, air-conditioned vehicle to the launch site, located approximately 30 minutes from the city center.
5:30–6:00 AM – Arrival at Launch Site & Pre-Flight Refreshments
Upon arrival, enjoy a warm welcome with Moroccan tea, coffee, and freshly baked croissants. Watch the fascinating process of balloon inflation as the sun begins to rise on the horizon.
6:00–7:00 AM – Takeoff & Flight
As dawn breaks, ascend into the sky and glide above the stunning landscapes of the North Palmeraie, traditional Berber villages, and the majestic foothills of the Atlas Mountains. The flight lasts approximately 40 to 60 minutes and offers breathtaking panoramic views.
7:00–8:00 AM – Landing & Traditional Berber Breakfast
After a gentle landing, you’ll be welcomed into a traditional tent for a delicious Berber breakfast. Enjoy Moroccan pancakes, fresh bread, olives, honey, and mint tea in a serene countryside setting.
8:00–9:00 AM – Certificate Presentation & Return Transfer
Receive a personalized flight certificate to commemorate your magical morning. Afterwards, relax as you're driven back to your hotel or riad in Marrakech, concluding this unforgettable experience.
