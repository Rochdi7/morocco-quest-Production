      Overview

      Embark on an inspiring 8-day Morocco luxury cultural tour, part of our curated morocco luxury tours collection, designed for travelers seeking authentic encounters, creativity, and cultural depth. This private guided experience reveals Morocco through its artisans, culinary heritage, and historic cities.

      Begin your journey in Marrakech, where you’ll be welcomed with an elegant dinner at Dar Zellij, one of the city’s most refined riad restaurants. Explore the vibrant Medina and its labyrinthine souks while engaging in hands-on artisan workshops, including wood-turning and embroidery, guided by skilled local craftspeople who preserve Morocco’s creative legacy.

      Continue to Rabat, Morocco’s coastal capital, where tradition and modernity coexist. Visit a women’s weaving cooperative, wander through the peaceful Kasbah des Oudaias, and enjoy a guided food tour that introduces the diverse flavors of the capital’s culinary scene. En route, explore the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis, a UNESCO World Heritage Site that offers insight into Morocco’s deep historical roots.

      Your journey concludes in Fez, the spiritual and cultural heart of Morocco. Dive into the UNESCO-listed Medina, participate in a traditional pottery workshop, and join an immersive cooking class at Palais Amani, learning to prepare authentic Moroccan dishes. Along the way, share lunch with locals in a Berber village and unwind in carefully selected riads that reflect Morocco’s elegance, warmth, and hospitality.

      Blending culture, craftsmanship, and gastronomy, this private guided tour in Morocco offers a meaningful and enriching journey for travelers seeking refined cultural discovery.

      Itinerary

      Expand all

      Welcome to Morocco! Upon your arrival in the vibrant city of Marrakech, you will be greeted by your friendly English-speaking driver, who will whisk you away to your charming riad nestled within the heart of the medina. After settling in and soaking up the unique atmosphere, prepare for an unforgettable evening at Dar Zellij, where you'll indulge in a sumptuous welcome dinner. Surrounded by stunning Moroccan decor, relish an exquisite array of traditional dishes, tantalizing your taste buds with rich spices and aromatic flavors while immersing yourself in the warm ambiance of this enchanting city.

      On your final day, relish a leisurely breakfast at your riad, allowing time for reflection on your transformative journey through Morocco's rich cultural landscape. As you prepare for your departure, cherish the memories of the vibrant traditions, warm hospitality, and artisanal crafts that have enriched your experience. Your English-speaking driver will transfer you to the airport, concluding your unforgettable cultural adventure in Morocco.

      Awake to the vibrant sights and sounds of Marrakech and set off on a captivating guided cultural tour. Discover the iconic Koutoubia Mosque, a magnificent landmark that embodies the beauty of Islamic architecture. Next, engage in hands-on experiences that connect you with local artisans: try your hand at wood turning with a master craftsman, creating a unique piece of art that embodies the essence of Moroccan culture. Visit the Annour Embroidery Association, where talented local women share their skills, showcasing the intricate artistry of traditional embroidery. After a delicious lunch at a charming riad, meander through the colorful souks, where the air is fragrant with spices, and the vibrant textiles and handicrafts offer a feast for the eyes.

      After a hearty breakfast, embark on a journey to Rabat, Morocco’s dignified capital. En route, visit a women’s weaving cooperative, where you'll gain insights into the age-old craft of weaving and the vital role it plays in empowering local women. Immerse yourself in the rich flavors of Moroccan cuisine with a food tour in the bustling medina, sampling local delicacies and experiencing the lively atmosphere of Rabat's markets. After a day filled with cultural exchanges, check into your hotel and enjoy dinner at a local restaurant, reflecting on the unique experiences of the day.

      Following breakfast, set off for Fez, the spiritual and cultural heart of Morocco. Along the way, visit the ancient ruins of Volubilis, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, where Roman history comes alive through its stunning mosaics and impressive architecture. Wander through the remnants of this once-thriving city, imagining the stories that echo through time. Arriving in Fez, check into your riad and savor dinner surrounded by the vibrant energy of this historic city.

      Today, delve into the mesmerizing depths of Fez’s rich cultural tapestry. Accompanied by a knowledgeable guide, wander through the labyrinthine streets of Fes el-Bali Medina, the world’s largest car-free urban area, and marvel at the architectural wonders of the Al-Qarawiyyin University and the Bou Inania Madrasa. Experience the vibrant souks, where artisans skillfully craft their wares, and witness the time-honored tradition of leather tanning at the famed tanneries. In the afternoon, participate in a pottery workshop, where local artisans will guide you in creating your own ceramic masterpiece, allowing you to connect with Morocco's rich artisan heritage. Enjoy a delicious dinner at your riad, sharing stories and laughter from a day of exploration.

      Start your day with a delightful breakfast before heading to Palais Amani for an immersive Moroccan cooking class. Under the guidance of expert chefs, you’ll learn the secrets of preparing traditional Moroccan dishes, using fresh, locally sourced ingredients that celebrate the region’s culinary heritage. After indulging in the delicious meal you've created for lunch, enjoy a leisurely afternoon exploring Fez's enchanting souks at your own pace. Visit local artisans, discover hidden gems, or simply relax in a café, sipping mint tea as you soak in the vibrant atmosphere. In the evening, delight in more exquisite Moroccan flavors at a local restaurant.

      After breakfast, embark on a scenic drive back to Marrakech, where the colorful sights and sounds of the city await. Upon arrival, the remainder of your day is yours to enjoy at leisure. Choose from a variety of optional activities, such as wandering through the stunning Majorelle Garden, exploring the Yves Saint Laurent Museum, or indulging in a revitalizing hammam experience. Enjoy dinner at a rooftop restaurant, where you can savor more of Morocco's culinary delights while gazing at the twinkling lights of the city as the sun sets.

      Accommodation

      Expand all

      Riad Dar Justo Hotel Boutique & Spa is a 14th-century riad nestled in the heart of Marrakech's medina, just a short walk from Jemaa el-Fna square. Meticulously restored by Spanish artist Justo Almendros, the riad artfully blends traditional Moroccan architecture with modern, eco-friendly comforts. Its 23 uniquely designed rooms and suites open onto serene patios, providing a tranquil retreat amidst the city's vibrant energy. Guests can unwind at Le Bain Bleu Spa, featuring hammams, jacuzzis, and a range of treatments using organic argan oil. The rooftop restaurant offers panoramic views of the Koutoubia Mosque and Atlas Mountains, serving authentic Moroccan and Mediterranean cuisine. With its harmonious fusion of history, art, and hospitality, Riad Dar Justo offers an immersive Moroccan experience.

      Riad Kalaa is a beautifully restored 19th-century nobleman's residence located in the heart of Rabat's medina. This traditional Moroccan guesthouse offers 11 individually decorated rooms and suites, each blending authentic craftsmanship with modern comforts. Guests can relax in the Andalusian patio, enjoy the rooftop terrace with a swimming pool, or indulge in the on-site spa and hammam. The riad's restaurant serves refined Moroccan cuisine, providing a culinary journey in an ambiance reminiscent of oriental palaces. Ideally situated near the Kasbah des Oudayas and the Atlantic Ocean, Riad Kalaa offers a serene retreat amidst the vibrant city life.

      Riad Myra is a beautifully restored early 20th-century residence located in the Batha district of Fez's medina. This elegant riad showcases Andalusian-Moorish architecture, featuring a central courtyard adorned with intricate stuccowork, colorful zellij tiles, and towering cedar wood doors. The 12 rooms and suites are individually decorated, blending traditional Moroccan craftsmanship with English antique furnishings, and are equipped with modern amenities such as air conditioning, satellite TV, and minibars. Guests can indulge in authentic Moroccan cuisine served in the courtyard or enjoy a drink at Moody's Bar, inspired by American culture. For relaxation, the riad offers a traditional hammam, massage services, and a rooftop terrace with views over the medina. Ideally situated near landmarks like Bab Boujloud and Medersa Bouanania, Riad Myra provides a serene retreat amidst the vibrant energy of Fez.

      Departing from Marrakech

      This tour departs from Marrakech — the Red City and Morocco's most dynamic imperial hub. Travellers fly into Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK), ideally arriving the day before departure for an early start. From central Marrakech, your private 4×4 heads south through the Tizi n'Tichka pass in the High Atlas, crossing the 2,260m col that separates the Atlantic-facing valleys from the pre-Saharan plateau. The visual contrast — from ochre medina walls to cedar forests to red gorges — begins within the first hour of driving. Browse all tours departing Marrakech.
