    Casablanca - Rabat - Chefchaouen

    Casablanca - Rabat - Chefchaouen
    Start point
    Casablanca, Casablanca-Settat, Morocco (Pick up from Hotel or Airport)
    Meals
    Breakfast

    (Casa Hassan)

    Included Activities

    • Guided tour of Hassan II Mosque interior including prayer hall, carved cedar ceilings, marble columns, zellige mosaics, ablution halls and hammam
    • Guided exploration of Kasbah of the Udayas including blue and white alleys and Andalusian gardens
    • Visit to Hassan Tower and Mausoleum of Mohammed V with views over ocean and old town of Salé

    • Visit to Al Quaraouiyine Mosque and University exterior
