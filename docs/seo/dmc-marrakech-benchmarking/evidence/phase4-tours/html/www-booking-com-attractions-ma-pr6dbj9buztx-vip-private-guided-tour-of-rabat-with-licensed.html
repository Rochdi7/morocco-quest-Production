Private Guided Walking Tour of Rabat with Licensed Tour Guide

Private full-day walking tour of Rabat with licensed guide including Kasbah of the Udayas and Hassan Tower

Step into Rabat’s historic heart on a private walking tour led by a licensed, expert guide. As you move through the city, you’ll explore the Kasbah of the Udayas with its characteristic blue-and-white streets and take in the sight of the imposing Hassan Tower. Along the way, your guide will share the history and cultural context behind these landmarks, helping you see different facets of the city’s past.

You’ll travel at a relaxed pace, with time to linger on details and follow up on the things that interest you most. Whether you’re a history buff, a lover of culture or simply curious, the experience will include local insights, discoveries and some lesser-known spots that often escape first-time visitors. This private format means the day will feel personal and unhurried, giving you space to absorb Rabat’s atmosphere and stories.
