Itinerary

Ourika Valley excursion from Marrakech
>> Daily departures <<

Pick up at your accommodations in Marrakech at 9:30 AM.

Head to the Ourika Valley (journey time approximately 1 hour). Located in the heart of the High Atlas Mountains, this valley is known for its impressive views and by the towns made of adobe houses hanging off the slopes of its mountains. With the help of a specialized mountain guide, we tour this place including the seven waterfalls that make up Setti Fatma. Free time for lunch.

On the return trip to the hustle and bustle of the medina of Marrakech, we make a stop at Anima Garden, a peaceful 1-hectare site created by artist André Heller filled with extravagant sculptures and numerous plants. Free time to explore (entrance fee included).

Pick up and transfer back to Marrakech, making a stop at  drop off at the accommodations at approximately 6:00pm. End of our services.

Total approximate duration of the activity: 8 hours.

Please don’t hesitate to inquire about any adjustment you want to make to this itinerary.
