Overview

 

Embark on a private full-day tour from Marrakech and discover four of the region’s most captivating attractions: the Water Museum (Optional: Entry Tickets not Included), Cactus Thiemann, Anima Garden, and Paradis du Safran. This experience offers a seamless blend of history, nature, art, and culture, with convenient pickup from your accommodation or a central meeting point.

You can choose to enjoy the tour with a certified professional guide (available in English or French) for a deeper cultural and historical understanding, or opt for a driver-only experience if you prefer to explore at your own pace. Please select your preferred option when booking.

Begin your journey at the Water Museum, where you’ll explore Morocco’s traditional irrigation techniques and learn about the significance of water in the country’s agricultural history.

Continue to Cactus Thiemann, one of Africa’s largest cactus gardens, home to over 150 species of cacti and succulents. Take time to admire the striking forms and textures of these unique plants in a peaceful setting.

Next, visit the imaginative Anima Garden by André Heller, a lush artistic oasis filled with vibrant sculptures, exotic plants, and panoramic views of the Atlas Mountains.

Finally, head to Paradis du Safran, a working saffron farm where you’ll learn about the cultivation of this valuable spice. Take part in a saffron tasting and browse natural products made on-site.

There will also be a lunch break (not included) in the beautiful Ourika Valley, where you can enjoy a meal with scenic mountain views before returning to Marrakech.
