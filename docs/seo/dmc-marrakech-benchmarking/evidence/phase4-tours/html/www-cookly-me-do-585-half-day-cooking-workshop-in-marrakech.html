What to Expect

In Keeping with its pioneering spirit in Morocco, the cooking workshops at La Maison Arabe take cooking experience to a different level and invite food lovers to discover the secrets of traditional Moroccan cuisine.

The cooking school is located in a beautifully restored space, just a few steps away from La Maison Arabe hotel, the classes are all run by a dada.

Today, La Maison Arabe is considered one of the leading gastronomic addresses in Marrakech.
