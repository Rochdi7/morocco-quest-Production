About your trip

To all wine lovers, this is for you! This tour will take you to the oldest winery in Morocco founded by the legendary Brahim Zniber. 


In the morning, your driver will pick you up from your hotel in Fes and take you to the Chateau Roselane winery, located 1 hour away from Fes.


Get ready to explore a captivating journey through the heart of Morocco's rich winemaking heritage during this tour. Embark on a full-day exploration that will awaken your senses and leave you with a profound appreciation for the artistry of wine.


Your adventure begins as we whisk you away to the prestigious Chateau Roselane Winery, nestled amidst the picturesque landscapes of Morocco. As you step foot onto the winery grounds, you'll be greeted by the timeless allure of vineyards stretching into the horizon, setting the stage for an unforgettable experience.


Embark on an immersive tour of the winery, led by knowledgeable guides who will unveil the secrets of winemaking that have been passed down through generations. Discover the intricate processes that transform plump grapes into the elixir of the gods, gaining insights into the meticulous craftsmanship that goes into each bottle.


But this journey isn't just about the science of winemaking; it's also a voyage through time. Immerse yourself in the captivating history of wine in Morocco, as you learn about its origins, evolution, and the pivotal role Brahim Zinber has played.


Prepare to tantalize your taste buds as we invite you to an exquisite lunch that harmoniously fuses the culinary delights of Moroccan cuisine with the perfect wine pairings. Delight in the symphony of flavors that dance upon your palate, as you savor every delectable bite amidst the rustic charm of the winery.


After the tour, the driver will take you back to your hotel.
