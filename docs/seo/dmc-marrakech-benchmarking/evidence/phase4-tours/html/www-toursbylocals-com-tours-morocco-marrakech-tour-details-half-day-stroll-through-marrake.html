Your Marrakech tour itinerary

Explore Marrakech starting in the historic Mouassine Quarter, known for its traditional architecture and lively local life. Step into the peaceful Secret Gardens, a hidden oasis away from the medina’s bustle. Discover the foundouks — historic inns that once served as trading hubs, now home to artisan workshops and shops. Visit El Heddadine, the blacksmith market where skilled artisans craft metalwork using traditional methods. Explore Rahba Lkdima, famous for its colorful spice and herb stalls. Admire the intricate tilework of Ben Youssef Madrasa, a 14th-century Islamic school. Experience the vibrant dyers market, where colorful fabrics are dyed using age-old techniques. Feel the energy of Jamaa El Fna Square, alive with storytellers, musicians, and food vendors. End your tour wandering the lively souks filled with textiles, pottery, and jewelry — perfect for shopping or soaking in the authentic atmosphere.
