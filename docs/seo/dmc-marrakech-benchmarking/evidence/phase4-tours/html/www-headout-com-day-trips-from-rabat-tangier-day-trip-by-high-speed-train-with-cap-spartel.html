    Getting started

    Your day begins at the train station in Rabat. Board Morocco's high-speed train for a comfortable journey to Tangier, where your local guide and driver welcome you on arrival. Before setting off, your guide gives a brief overview of the day's route, covering Cape Spartel, Hercules Caves, the Kasbah, the Medina, and Grand Socco.

    What to expect

    High-speed journey to Tangier

    Start your experience with one of Morocco's fastest rail journeys, travelling comfortably from Rabat to Tangier before your guided exploration begins.

    Features

    • Travel aboard Morocco's modern high-speed train, offering a smooth and relaxing journey between Rabat and Tangier.

    • Transfers in Tangier are included, making the experience seamless from start to finish.

    • Meet your local guide and driver upon arrival, ready to lead you through Tangier's top attractions.

    Cape Spartel

    Your first sightseeing stop is Cape Spartel, where dramatic coastal scenery marks the meeting point of the Atlantic Ocean and the Strait of Gibraltar.

    Features

    • Take in sweeping ocean views from one of Tangier's most scenic viewpoints and enjoy time for photographs.

    • Learn about the strategic importance of this coastal landmark through your guide's commentary.

    • Admire the rugged coastline before continuing to your next stop.

    Hercules Caves

    Continue to the legendary Hercules Caves, one of Tangier's most famous natural attractions.

    Features

    • Explore the cave's accessible areas while your guide shares the myths and legends connected to Hercules.

    • View the cave's iconic sea-facing opening and discover why it remains one of the city's most photographed landmarks.

    • Enjoy free time to capture photos before returning to your vehicle.

    Kasbah

    Head into Tangier's historic Kasbah for a guided walk through its charming streets and centuries-old neighbourhoods.

    Features

    • Walk through narrow lanes lined with traditional buildings as your guide explains the Kasbah's history and significance.

    • Stop at scenic viewpoints overlooking the city and coastline.

    • Discover local architecture and everyday life within this historic district.

    Medina

    From the Kasbah, continue directly into Tangier's bustling old Medina.

    Features

    • Wander through colourful streets filled with traditional markets, artisan shops, and local cafés.

    • Learn about the Medina's role as Tangier's historic commercial centre from your guide.

    • Enjoy time to browse for souvenirs and take photographs before regrouping.

    Grand Socco

    Visit Grand Socco, the vibrant square connecting Tangier's old town with its modern city.

    Features

    • Experience one of Tangier's busiest public spaces, surrounded by cafés, shops, and historic buildings.

    • Hear stories about the square's role in the city's cultural and social life.

    • Take a final stroll and capture a few last photographs before your return journey.

    Wrapping up

    After exploring Tangier's coastal landmarks and historic neighbourhoods, return to the train station for your high-speed journey back to Rabat. Your full-day guided experience comes to a comfortable close.
