We will pick you up at your accommodation to start this Chefchaouen Private Walking Tour. Chefchaouen is a city that stands out for its buildings in blue tones, which has given it the nickname of "Blue City". An expert guide will guide you through the city while you learn about its fascinating history and culture, visiting the bustling markets and appreciating the stunning views. As you walk through the streets, your guide will tell you all about the history and culture of the city. The private guided tour service is designed to cater to your individual preferences, whether you are interested in learning about the city's art and architecture, trying local delicacies or simply strolling through its picturesque streets, your guide will show you all the places that are of interest to you.


*Pickup available for accommodation inside the Medina of Chefchaouen. If you are staying in hotels outside the Medina or Airbnb, we will set a meeting point.
