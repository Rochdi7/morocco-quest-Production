DMC Marrakech - Marrakech, the city of roses, is a destination that should definitely be on your bucket list. Picture palace restaurants with bubbling water fountains, mosaic walls and rose petals scattered on table cloths where the warm air is filled with the scent of cinnamon and incense. The people still travel by donkey through the city, snake charmers perform their dance on the market and the bustling souks invite passing strangers to buy silver jewelry and hand-woven carpets.

Marrakech is blessed with the most stunning hotels, historic riads and amazing restaurants where you can feast on traditional tagines and couscous. Marrakech is very easy to combine with a desert tour, where you take a camel ride and spend your night in a luxury tent under a star lit sky.

Travel highlights

 Medina Souks

Shopping is a treat in the beautiful souks. Marrakech's labyrinthine medina, which is the old city district is a must-see

Djemaa El Fna

is a vibrant market place of bric-a-brac stalls, musicians, storytellers, fortune-tellers, and snake charmers that come to life each day with entertainment carrying on until midnight. A truly Moroccan experience.

Koutoubia Mosque
The Koutoubia Mosque is Marrakesh's most famous landmark with its striking, 70-meter-tall minaret visible for miles in every direction.

 Visit the Hammam
Some of the more luxurious hammams also provide modern spa add-ons with a host of beauty treatments and massages available.
