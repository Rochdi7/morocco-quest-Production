## Provenance
- **No AI-generated images, no newly downloaded stock, no logos.**
- Every new file is a resized/cropped derivative of an image **already published on morocco-quest.com** (`assets/img/…`). It was generated with Pillow (Lanczos resampling, WebP quality 78, method 6) by `evidence/phase3/make_images.py` (run from the repo root: `python docs/seo/dmc-marrakech-benchmarking/evidence/phase3/make_images.py /tmp/manifest.json`; manifest saved as `evidence/phase3/image_manifest.json`).
- The originals' licences are **not recorded in the repo**. The owner must confirm rights before deploy (report 19, "Image provenance").
- **None of these images documents a Morocco Quest event.** Alt text describes only what is visible and never claims a programme was delivered by MQ.
- The three sample-programme images sit under an on-page disclaimer that the formats are illustrative.

Every source image was **visually checked**. Four candidates were rejected because their filenames or alt text described a different picture, or because they showed third-party branding (see §3).
