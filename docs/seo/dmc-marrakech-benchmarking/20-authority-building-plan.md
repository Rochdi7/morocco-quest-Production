**Legitimate review strategy (B2B clients):**
1. After each delivered programme, send the agency or planner contact a short thank-you email with the direct review link (GBP "Ask for reviews" link). Ask once and send at most one reminder.
2. Ask **every** client, not only happy ones. Never offer discounts or gifts for reviews (against Google's policy).
3. Invite group leaders and private clients through the same link. Tripadvisor ("Morocco Quest Dmc") is the second destination.
4. Reply to every review within a week, in the reviewer's language.
5. Track monthly: review count, rating, and whether MQ appears in the "dmc marrakech" map pack (report 23, KPI list).
