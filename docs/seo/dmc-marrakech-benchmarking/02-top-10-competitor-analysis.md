## Individual analysis

### 1. mice.visitmarrakech.com — the Marrakech tourism board's DMC list
- **Why it ranks:** it's the official convention-bureau directory of Marrakech DMCs, a government entity with high trust and exact topical relevance.
- **What it means for us:** this is a **listing**, not a rival. If Morocco Quest is eligible (membership in the Conseil Régional du Tourisme de Marrakech / a licensed agency), being listed here puts the brand on the #1 result and earns a highly relevant .ma link. **Highest-value citation in this SERP.**

### 2. dmc-marrakech.com (MidFar DMC)
- Title: `Home` (!) · Meta description: none · Canonical: none · H1: `DMC Marrakech` · H3s: "DMC Marrakech", "DMC Services Marrakech", "About MidFar DMC Marrakech", "Travel facts Morocco".
- Words: **761** · Schema: Organization, WebSite, WebPage, Article · 3 of 6 images without alt text.
- Semrush: AS 6, 2,324 referring domains (RDs), of which 2,185 are AS 0–2.
- **Strengths:** the domain *is* the query, the H1 is the exact phrase, and it's part of a DMC network (MidFar, dmcprofessionals.com) that cross-links.
- **Weaknesses:** almost every on-page basic is broken. It wins on domain and anchor match, not quality.

### 3. attendance.ma
- SERP title: `DMC Marrakech Morocco - MICE`. Homepage. Semrush AS 4, 1,111 RDs but 38,167 backlinks (heavy sitewide linking).
- Marrakech-based, MICE positioning. Page couldn't be fetched (connection reset), so no on-page detail.

### 4. your-morocco-dmc.com
- Title: `Your Morocco DMC, Marrakech destination management company` · H1: `YOUR MOROCCO DMC THE POWER OF LOCAL KNOWLEDGE`.

## What the winners share

1. **The ranking URL is a homepage of a DMC-only site** (7 of the top 8 in Morocco). The whole domain is about destination management, so topical focus is total.
2. **"DMC" in the domain name** for 6 of the top 8 (dmc-marrakech, dmcmarrakech, your-morocco-dmc, boardingpassdmc, globaldmcpartners, plus attendance.ma whose brand is "Attendance DMC").
3. **The exact phrase "DMC Marrakech" leads the title or H1** on every dedicated page that ranks.
4. **Proof of being a real, long-running operator:** "since 1994", "20+ years", client logos, testimonials, Google Business Profiles with ratings, TripAdvisor listings.
5. **Not content length.** Top-8 page sizes run from 447 to about 1,566 words (nolimits about 3,000). Morocco Quest's 3,700 words is already the longest. Length is not the gap.
6. **Not raw backlink counts.** Every competitor's Semrush profile looks like Morocco Quest's: about 2,100–2,500 RDs, over 90% of them Authority Score 0–2. The difference is a few relevant links (directory, network and tourism-board citations) and, most of all, **domain-level relevance**.

## Weaknesses competitors leave open

- Weak on-page basics (missing titles and descriptions on #2, missing alt text everywhere, little schema). Morocco Quest is technically ahead.
- Little explicit B2B process detail (net rates, quote turnaround, white-label). Morocco Quest covers this better than anyone.
- Few FAQs (only dmcmarrakech, originallymorocco and themoroccoway have them).
